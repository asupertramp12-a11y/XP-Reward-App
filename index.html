📖 Douglas Battle Pass Guide

🌟 Battle Pass Rules

The goal is not to be perfect. The goal is to try your best every day, help your family, learn new things and look after yourself.

🏡 Monday - OWS Day

Every Monday is OWS Day (Our Wilde Space). Douglas spends the day with Mum at OWS and naturally completes lots of learning, creativity, outdoor adventures and social activities.

OWS Days are all about exploring, building confidence, creating, learning and having fun together.

🎯 Mission Objective

Complete daily missions throughout the week to earn Battle Stars, Screen Tokens and level up your Battle Pass.

⭐ Battle Stars

Every completed activity earns a Battle Star. There are 42 available each week.

🎟 Screen Tokens

Learning and challenge-based activities award Screen Tokens which can be exchanged in the Reward Shop.

🎮 Gaming Rules

  • Gaming is normally after 4:00pm.
  • No gaming before daily missions are completed.
  • No gaming in the morning.
  • Gaming is earned through positive effort and participation.
  • Gaming should stop promptly when Mum or Dad ask.
  • If Douglas is tired, overwhelmed or struggling emotionally, gaming may wait until he is calm and ready.
  • Real life always comes before screen time.

🤝 Team Rules

  • Be kind to Mum, Dad and Dorothea.
  • Use calm words, even when frustrated.
  • Treat family members like teammates.
  • Help others when they need support.
  • Listen when someone is speaking.
  • Everyone makes mistakes. Heroes learn, reset and try again.

💜 Looking After Dorothea

  • Dorothea is on Douglas's team.
  • Use gentle words and gentle hands.
  • Protect younger teammates.
  • Being patient with Dorothea is a Hero skill.
  • If feeling annoyed or frustrated, step away and ask Mum or Dad for help.
  • Heroes never take out anger on teammates.

😌 Managing Big Feelings

  • Feeling angry is OK.
  • Feeling frustrated is OK.
  • Feeling tired is OK.
  • Feeling sad is OK.
  • Hurting people or speaking unkindly is not OK.
  • Take a break when feelings get too big.
  • Use breathing, movement or quiet time to reset.
  • Heroes ask for help when they need it.

💪 Hero Habits

  • Try your best.
  • Complete missions.
  • Keep learning.
  • Spend time outdoors.
  • Help your family.
  • Look after your mind and body.
